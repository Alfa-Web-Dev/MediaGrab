# Privacy Policy (template: edit and have it reviewed before publishing)

Last updated: <date>

MediaGrab ("the app") runs on your device. It has no user accounts and no server of its own.

**What stays on your device**
- Links you open or paste, your download history, and your settings.
- Files you download, saved to your Downloads folder.

**What the app sends over the internet**
- Requests to the websites you choose to open or download from (the same as a web browser would send).
- Requests to GitHub to update the download engine (yt-dlp), if you use "Update engine".
- Icon images for the site shortcuts are loaded from a public favicon service.

**What we do not do**
- We do not collect, sell or share personal data. We do not run analytics or advertising in this build.

**Permissions**
- Internet: to browse and download. Notifications: to show download progress.
- Foreground service: to keep downloads running when the app is closed.

**Your responsibility**
You are responsible for downloading only content you own or have permission to save, and for following
each website's terms and the laws that apply to you.
